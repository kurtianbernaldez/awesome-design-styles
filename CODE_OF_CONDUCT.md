# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project is expected to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Focus feedback on the work, assume good intent, and make room for different backgrounds and perspectives.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and publishing someone else’s private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.
